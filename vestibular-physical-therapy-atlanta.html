Finding vestibular physical therapy in Atlanta

Vestibular physical therapy is a specialized form of rehabilitation that can help people who are navigating dizziness, vertigo, imbalance, motion sensitivity, or fall concerns.

What to look for

When searching for an Atlanta vestibular PT, ask about the clinician’s experience with your symptoms, their approach to balance and fall-risk assessment, and whether they have advanced neurologic or vestibular training. A consistent care relationship can also matter when symptoms change over time.

Search Atlanta providers

The Vestibular Disorders Association maintains a healthcare directory that can help you find vestibular specialists near Atlanta. Review provider profiles, then contact the clinic to confirm appointment availability and fit.
