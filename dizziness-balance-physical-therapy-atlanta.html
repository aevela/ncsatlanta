Dizziness and balance physical therapy in Atlanta

Dizziness and balance changes can affect confidence, walking, community activities, and fall risk. A physical therapy evaluation can help identify movement and balance goals for rehabilitation.

Preparing for a visit

Before your appointment, write down when symptoms began, what triggers them, recent falls or near-falls, medications, and any relevant neurologic or ear-related diagnoses. This gives the clinician a clearer starting point for the evaluation.

Choosing the right fit

Look for a clinic that regularly treats dizziness and balance concerns. Ask whether your PT has experience with vestibular rehabilitation, falls prevention, neurologic conditions, and the goals most important to your daily life.
