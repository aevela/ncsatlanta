The NCS difference

More than a license.
Deeper neuro expertise.

All physical therapists are trained to help people move better. An NCS is a licensed PT who has gone further—earning advanced board certification in neurologic physical therapy through the American Board of Physical Therapy Specialties. Learn about NCS certification ↗

Know the credentials

DPT or master’s PT?
Why advanced education matters.

Traditional master’s-level physical therapy programs are two years long and often provide less clinical experience compared with doctoral pathways. The shorter format offers less time for advanced training in clinical decision-making, specialization, and hands-on preparation for complex patient needs.

The Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) places a greater focus on clinical training, examination and diagnosis, evidence-informed treatment planning, specialty practice, and preparing PTs to stay current with emerging research in physical therapy.

For neurologic and vestibular conditions, choosing a DPT with advanced specialty training—especially a board-certified NCS—can mean more specialized evaluation, more precise treatment planning, and care grounded in the latest understanding of how the nervous system affects movement, balance, and recovery.
